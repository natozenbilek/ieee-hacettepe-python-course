Latin Modern Roman 10 (regular, bold, italic), the Computer Modern family as digitised by the
GUST e-foundry. Used on the site for the title "Introduction to Python" and the Tarihçe page
(authoring/DECISIONS.md "Computer Modern for Tarihçe and the hero title").

Source files: lmroman10-regular.otf, lmroman10-bold.otf and lmroman10-italic.otf, version 2.004,
from the TeX Live "lm" package. Changes: subset to Basic Latin, Latin-1, Latin Extended-A and common
punctuation (the Turkish letters included) and saved as WOFF with fontTools 4.65. The font names
inside the files are unchanged; the files are renamed latin-modern-roman-10-<style>.woff.
